Why can the predicted radius decrease?

The learned error model is not monotonic in station count. Removing a reading changes the joint temperature, source disagreement and error-model inputs. It can therefore predict a narrower interval. This is not proof of improved accuracy or evidence that the station is bad: the model did not achieve its 90% nightly coverage target. Blue means a smaller predicted radius, not a validated benefit. We retain these outputs rather than forcing every removal to increase uncertainty.
